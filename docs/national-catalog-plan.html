      1. Coverage goals, required highlights, and actual counts

      The validator and date regressions exist. Validate every manifest year. Replace full-season ranges with separate openers and title-deciding closing highlights. All-Star extras cannot replace either anchor. Keep a Final Four/Final Six as one multi-day event.

      Use stable sport and competition keys. Read TSV fields by name. Merge edition identities and retain evidence. Add each country's national events. Verify country or international scope; classify or exclude unresolved events before Ready and retain them for Preview review.
