# Gate Ready on required highlights

**Status:** Accepted

The coverage part of a catalog year's `Ready` gate requires every configured required highlight for that year to be present with a verified date and source. Only an official organizer, federation, or competition source can establish its verified date or full date range for the relevant edition and stage. Secondary sources support discovery and cross-checks but leave a requirement pending without official confirmation. Optional highlights may be absent or pending and do not block `Ready`. This makes the evidence requirement clear without requiring every possible event; other release checks remain separate.
