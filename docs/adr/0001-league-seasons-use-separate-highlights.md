# Represent league seasons as separate highlights

**Status:** Accepted

A league season is a competition edition that groups separate, date-verified highlights. The opener and closing highlight are separate events. The closing highlight is the title-deciding game, series, or multi-day final tournament, such as a Final Four or Final Six; a multi-day final tournament is one event over its official date range, not a list of individual games. If a league has no separate playoff final, its final matchday can close the season; a routine last regular-season matchday does not count when playoffs decide the title. Other verified highlights, such as an All-Star game, may be added as separate events but cannot replace either anchor. Do not store the full league season as one continuous event. This keeps each calendar choice precise and makes required stages visible.
