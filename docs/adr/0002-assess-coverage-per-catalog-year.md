# Assess coverage per catalog year

**Status:** Accepted

Assess required highlight coverage separately for each calendar-year catalog and bind it to event dates. Require every configured highlight that falls in a year to have a verified date and source in that year's coverage report. A competition season can have its opener in one catalog year and its closing highlight in the next; do not combine both years into one season-wide coverage result. This matches the yearly catalog users download and keeps the coverage report tied to the events available in that year.
