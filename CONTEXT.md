# Sport Event Catalog

This glossary defines the competition and event terms used by the sport calendar.

## Competition model

**Catalog year**: A calendar-year view of events whose date ranges overlap that year. Coverage is assessed per catalog year and follows event dates, even when a competition season spans more than one year.

**Competition season**: One edition of a recurring competition, such as 2026–27. It can contain several dated highlights. It is not one continuous event.

**Season highlight**: One separately selectable, source-backed event within a competition season. It has its own identity and dates. The opener and closing highlight are distinct events. Other verified highlights, such as an All-Star game, are optional additions and do not replace either one.

**Season closing highlight**: The title-deciding end of a league season. Use its final game, final series, or multi-day final tournament, such as a Final Four or Final Six. If the league has no separate playoff final, its final matchday can close the season. A routine final regular-season matchday does not count when playoffs decide the title.

**Multi-day final event**: One closing highlight with an official date range, such as a Final Four or Final Six. It is one calendar event, not a list of its individual games.

**Required highlight**: A named event that must have a verified date and source in its catalog year. Optional highlights do not satisfy or block a required highlight.

**Official date source**: A source published by the event organizer, responsible federation, or competition that explicitly confirms the event date or full date range for the relevant edition and stage.

**Verified required-highlight date**: A required highlight's date or full date range confirmed by an official date source. Secondary sources alone do not establish this status.

**Coverage goal**: A desired area of catalog breadth, such as football, basketball, or athletics in a target country. A coverage goal is not a release requirement by itself.

## Audience scope

**National event**: An event assigned to one or more target countries. The country filter controls its visibility. If it has several country tags, selecting any one of them makes it visible.

**International event**: An event assigned shared international scope. It remains visible for every country selection.

**Unresolved audience scope**: An event with no verified country assignment and no verified international scope. It can remain in Preview, but it cannot appear in a Ready catalog.

**Women's top league**: The leading domestic league for women in a sport and target country. The coverage list names which competitions meet this term.
