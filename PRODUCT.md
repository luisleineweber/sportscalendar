# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sports fans who select events across sports and export a personal all-day calendar. The maintainer updates source data with a local monthly script.

## Product Purpose

Provide correct dates for major international events and national league seasons, cup finals, and senior championships in Germany, the USA, the UK, France, and Italy. Keep the website static and the export local.

## Brand Personality

Direct, compact, practical. Preserve the existing Sportkalender structure and visual identity, as requested by the user.

## Anti-references

Do not replace the existing Sports panel, Events panel, and export dock with a country-first portal. Do not imply full league schedules or complete country coverage when the data does not support them.

## Design Principles

- Keep sport as the main filter and event-list order.
- Keep event choices in the Events panel. Use competition and season names only as event context.
- Filter national events by selected country. Verified international events remain eligible. Show unresolved audience scope as a separate Preview review class.
- Represent league seasons with dated openers, finals, and other highlights. Do not add one event for the full season.
- Preserve event choices when country or sport filters change.
- Show actual row counts and data gaps separately from planned coverage.
- Separate catalog year, competition season, and event identity.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Keep labelled native controls, visible keyboard focus, clear selected and mixed states, and usable layouts on narrow screens. Preserve the current focus behavior. The detailed plan defines verification checks; this document is not an accessibility certification.
